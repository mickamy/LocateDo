StopBy Support

Help for StopBy (ちかメモ in Japanese), the to-do app that reminds you when you arrive at a place.

Reminders don’t arrive when the app is closed

  • In iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > StopBy, choose “Always” and turn on Precise Location.
  • In iOS Settings > Notifications > StopBy, allow notifications.
  • iOS can take a few minutes to notice an arrival, and small areas are noticed less reliably. A notification radius of 100 m (330 ft) or more works best.

Use StopBy on more than one device

Open Settings > Account and sign in with Apple, using the same Apple ID on each device. Your places, to-dos, and categories stay in sync.

Share with your family

Sharing needs StopBy Pro on the household owner’s account; family members join for free. Tap the people icon on the Home tab, choose Invite, and send the link. A link can be used once and works for 72 hours. A household holds up to 6 people.

Restore or cancel StopBy Pro

  • To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings > StopBy Pro > Restore Purchases.
  • To cancel, open Settings > StopBy Pro > Manage Subscription, or your Apple ID’s Subscriptions in iOS Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

Delete your account

Open Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes your account from our servers; if you own a household, the household and everything in it are removed too. Without an account, your data lives only on your device and is deleted with the app.

Contact us

Email support@locatedo.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (Settings > About > Version).
