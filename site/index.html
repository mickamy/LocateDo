Reminders when you arrive

Arrive at the store and get a notification with what you need there, even with the app closed. Check things off right from the notification.

A list for every store

The grocery store, the drugstore, the hardware store. Write things down where you'll buy them. Share the lists with your family, and assign a to-do so only that person is reminded when they arrive.

Know when your family picks it up

When someone in your family checks something off, you get a notification, so nobody buys it twice. Family sharing is part of Pro.

Your location stays on your phone

Arrivals are detected on your phone; your current location is never sent to our servers. No ads, no tracking.

Free to start

Up to 3 places and 15 open to-dos are free. Pro removes the limits and lets you share with your family, up to 6 people. Choose monthly, or yearly with a one-week free trial.
