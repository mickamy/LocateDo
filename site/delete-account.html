Delete your StopBy account

StopBy (ちかメモ in Japanese). You can also delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and your devices’ registrations on our servers.
  • If you own a household, the household and all of its places, to-dos, and categories, for everyone in it.
  • If you belong to someone else’s household, you leave it, and what you added stays with that household.

What is kept

  • Database backups are removed within 70 days, and server logs after 30 days.
  • Google keeps usage analytics for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.
  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel StopBy Pro. Cancel it in the App Store (iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

Sign in to delete

Sign in the same way you did in the app: with Apple on iPhone, with Google on Android. We only use the sign-in to find your account.
