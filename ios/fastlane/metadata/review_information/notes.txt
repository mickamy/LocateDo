StopBy reminds you of to-dos when you arrive at a place you saved, such as "buy milk" at the grocery store, and lets a household share those lists.

LOCATION ("ALWAYS" PERMISSION)
Arrival reminders must work while the app is closed, which is the whole point of the app, so it asks for "Always" location after first asking for "While Using". Arrival detection runs on the device with iOS region monitoring (CLMonitor) around the places you saved; the current location is never sent to our servers or anyone else and is not stored as a history. The app still works with "While Using", but reminders then only fire while it is open; Settings explains this and links to iOS Settings.

HOW TO TRY IT
1. Home > Add a place: pick a point on the map (or use the current location) and save it.
2. Open the place and add a to-do.
3. When the device enters the place's radius, a notification lists the open to-dos there.

ACCOUNT (OPTIONAL)
No account is needed for the main features; everything is stored on the device. Sign in with Apple (Settings > Account) adds sync across devices and family sharing. No demo account is needed: any Apple ID can sign in. Account deletion: Settings > Account > Delete Account; it also revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

STOPBY PRO (AUTO-RENEWABLE SUBSCRIPTION)
Free: 3 places and 15 open to-dos. Pro (yearly with a one-week free trial, or monthly) removes the limits and lets the owner share with up to 5 family members, who use Pro for free. The paywall opens from Settings > StopBy Pro, from the sharing screen, or when a free limit is reached. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the paywall. Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and the Privacy Policy are linked on the paywall.

FAMILY SHARING
Testing this needs two Apple IDs. As a Pro owner: Home > Share (top left) > Invite, then open the link on a second device signed in with another Apple ID. Members see the same places and to-dos, and a to-do can be assigned to one member so only they are reminded.
