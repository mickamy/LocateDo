Never forget the milk again. StopBy reminds you what to buy the moment you arrive at the store, and shares the list with your family.

Save the places you go, like the grocery store, the drugstore, or the hardware store, and add what you need at each one. When you arrive, StopBy sends a notification with that place's open to-dos. No more remembering only after you get home.

WHY STOPBY
• Reminders by place, not by time, so they come when you can act on them
• Works while the app is closed, using location on your phone
• A list for every store, with categories you can make your own
• A map and a nearby list show what is waiting where

SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY (PRO)
• One shared list for the household: anything anyone adds reaches everyone's phone
• Assign a to-do to one person, and only they are reminded when they arrive
• One subscription covers the household, and family members join for free

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Arrivals are detected on your phone, and your location history is never stored
• Use it without an account; sign in with Google only to sync and share
• No ads and no tracking

FREE AND PRO
Free: up to 3 places and 15 open to-dos.
StopBy Pro removes the limits and adds family sharing for up to 6 people. Choose yearly, with a one-week free trial, or monthly.

Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
